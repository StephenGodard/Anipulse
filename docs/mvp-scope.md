# MVP Scope

## Core loop

AniPulse runs daily and creates a small content batch in Notion.

Daily target:

- 1 SEO/article idea when relevant;
- 1 to 2 X/Twitter posts;
- 1 TikTok/Reels script;
- 0 to 1 bonus Reddit, Instagram, or LinkedIn item.

## Default status

Every generated content item must be created as `A valider`.

## Human control

AniPulse does not publish. Stephen validates and posts manually.

## Out of scope

- automated posting;
- automated use of anime screenshots without review;
- video generation as a blocking dependency;
- large-scale content generation.
